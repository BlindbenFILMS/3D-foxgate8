RULES
Run hoops 1 to 6 in order, the way the painted arrow points. Then hit the striped peg to win.
Run your hoop: one extra stroke. Hit another ball (a roquet): two extra strokes, once per ball until your next hoop. Out of bounds ends your turn.
Score: 10 per hoop, 30 for the peg, minus 1 per stroke.
CONTROLS
Drag back from anywhere and let go to hit. Tap the lawn to aim at a spot. Or hold STRIKE and release on the power you want.
Keyboard: arrows aim and set power, Space to start and stop the meter, V for the court view.
