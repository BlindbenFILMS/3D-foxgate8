HOW TO PLAYAim, wobble, pop
Drag anywhere to aim. The ring sits just above your finger and wobbles more the longer you hold. Let go to throw.
Every balloon is 10. Gold "50" pays 50. Pink star pays 100. Black blast pops its 4 neighbours. Ice snowflake stops the wobble for 5 s. White plus gives one more dart.
3 hits in a row doubles your points, 5 in a row triples them. A miss resets it.
Assist pulls the ring onto the nearest balloon and plays a tone when a balloon is under it.
