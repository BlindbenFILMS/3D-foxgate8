HOW TO PLAY Four moves per stone
1
SET THE BROOMTap or drag on the ice. The stone starts toward the broom. ‹ › fine-tune it.
2
PICK THE CURLThe stone bends that way, most of all as it slows down. Aim the broom to the other side.
3
HOLD THROWThe weight bar fills. Let go in GUARD (stops short), HOUSE (stops in the rings) or HIT (knocks stones out). Or tap once to start the bar and tap again to throw.
4
SWEEPHold SWEEP, or hold anywhere on the ice, while your stone runs. It goes further and curls less.
★
SCORINGWhen every stone is thrown, whoever is closest to the button scores one point for each of their stones closer than anyone else’s best. A stone must pass the far red line, and stones past the back line or touching the side are out.
