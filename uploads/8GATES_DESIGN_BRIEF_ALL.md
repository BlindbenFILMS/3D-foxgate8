# 8 GATES — Claude Design World Brief (all 11 worlds)

## Overnight job

Build all 11 worlds of 8 GATES in 3D, one at a time, in this order: Meru, Gaya, Jidda, Kufa, Luxor, Nebo, Ur, Zion, Home planet, Earth, Deep Space Fox station.

- Finish each world completely, every room listed, before starting the next. Save each world as its own file named after it (MERU, GAYA, ...) so nothing is lost if you run out of room.
- Use the Meru kit for every world (avatars, weapons in hand, fight system, dialogue, phone controls, time of day). Don't redesign the kit per world, only the places.
- Keep every NPC name, line and room name in brackets exactly as written.
- If you have to cut something, cut decoration, never a listed room, NPC or minigame.
- At the end, write a short list of what is finished, what is partial, and what you didn't get to.

## Shared rules (apply to every world)

Use the same kit as Meru for every world: the polished fox avatars, weapons in hand, the fight system, the dialogue box, the phone joystick with buttons 1 and 2, the quest banner, and the time-of-day light. Read the game's HTML for each world and keep every NPC name, line of dialogue, quest step, shop item and price exactly as written there. Every room listed must exist, with its doors leading where it says. The things listed in each room are the must-haves the game uses; fill in everything else (decor, lights, signs, crowds) the way you did the Meru square. Keep it light enough to run smoothly on an iPhone.

How each room reads: the room's name, then what is in it, then who is there, then where its doors go. Words in CAPITALS are things the player uses (a minigame, a shop, a quest object). The name in brackets (like meruTavern) is the game's own room name: keep it exactly, so the rooms can be matched back to the game files.

Every world except Earth also has a SPACE DOCK in orbit (the ship flies in and docks, a TELEPORT pad down to the planet, the ship back to SPACE), a RETURN TO ORBIT button, and the Gaya monks' loading screens.

## Meru

**Story in one breath:** Meru is the centre of the Nine, and King Might is hosting his own birthday tournament in the Arena (the prize is the STAFF). Mid-party the Machine Fleet malfunctions and attacks the Meru transport, so you land on the bridge and hold it; his missing son Max is found caged by bandits in the ruins caves.

## Jidda

## Kufa

Water is currency: standpipes, jars, tally boards, one oasis, one cold room. Underground, a cold dry spy station with lasers and a green-then-red arch.

**Story in one breath:** Nine wells on the west road are failing in order, and the water broker's count does not add up. Escort the caravans west to the Way Station, break the siphon rig on the pipe head, then go under the oasis to Station 9 and face the Plated Coat (KASPER) at the Berth.
